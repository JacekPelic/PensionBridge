● The load-bearing assumptions — where to spend all the diligence

  • Entitlement rate (LU per-community 30–70% & EU 50%) — unsourced, and the single biggest driver of the headline. Ground it with revealed data: HIVA corridor-level cross-border-pension counts + age-at-immigration.
  • The "other foreign 118K" bucket (LU) — a heterogeneous catch-all on a blended guess; widest single swing in the LU number.
  • The LU nationals layer — softest input; needs naturalized-by-origin data.
  • Not load-bearing: the age shares (~40% / ~45%) — interpolated but bounded and cheaply verifiable from STATEC LUSTAT and the EC report. Don't waste diligence here.

How to read this ledger

Two different claims, graded separately. The existence of the market rests on facts (Section C is observed behavior, not a model — the strong hand). The precise size rests on three assumptions, flagged ●. So the honest position is: the order of magnitude is real; the point estimate is a band, not a number.

Proven (facts)
Population scale (LU 322K foreign; EU 10.1M movers) and that the pain is real & money is observably on the table (CHF 6bn unclaimed; millions of dashboard logins).
A band, not a point
LU TAM 60–90K · EU TAM ~2–3M (1.8–3.6M). The width comes almost entirely from the ● assumptions.
Not yet proven
Willingness to pay and reachability at a viable CAC — the real funding gate, and a real-world test, not a spreadsheet.
