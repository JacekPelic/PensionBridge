The swing factor. The estimate turns mostly on one assumption: the share of each community that worked in their home country and built a real pension there before moving. It matters most for the Portuguese community — Luxembourg’s largest, and historically young, low-skill emigration, so “born in Portugal” does not mean “has a Portuguese pension to reconcile.” Replacing this judgement with the underlying STATEC age-by-nationality data is the top open item.

Why our number is lower than the earlier one

EstimateEarlier (exploratory)Current (graded)What changed
Luxembourg TAM 130–150K 60–90K Filters cut it (age 35–60 + genuine entitlement); the nationals layer adds some back
Realistic yearly outcome — 150–400 Sized the reachable flow, not just the static pool

The earlier figure was an all-ages, generous-entitlement count of foreign residents only, presented as the addressable market. We narrow it with two filters, then widen it back by including Luxembourg nationals with foreign careers (foreign residents alone are ~45–65K). The two numbers measure different things, not a contradiction. Luxembourg on its own is a proof market, not the venture-scale prize; that comes from the wider EU corridor build-out and the capital opportunity.

Confidence grading

verified primary source / first-party data estimate reasoned, ranged, not sourced assumption a bet we carry until tested open known unknown, flagged for work
  • Verified: all resident populations (STATEC, Jan 2026); annual arrivals (~25,700/yr); EU-institution, Big-Four and finance-sector headcounts.
  • Estimate: the 35–60 age share (~40%) and the per-community entitlement ratios — the latter is the main lever on the headline number.
  • Assumption: the trigger-activation rate that converts static stock into yearly flow.
  • Open: real STATEC age-by-nationality data (to replace the entitlement estimate); naturalized-by-origin and return-migration data (to firm up the nationals layer — currently the softest part); and conversion / willingness-to-pay (blocked until price is decided — this model is deliberately price-free).
