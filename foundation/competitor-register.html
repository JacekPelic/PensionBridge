mypension.be BEGovt dashboard ✕Free ●●● free anchor Strongest anchor of all four: ~33% of adults, doubling; P1+P2 (+reportedly P3). But amount forecasting & simulation paused to H2 2027 (pension reform), and no cross-border aggregation. Real, time-boxed window.
Easyvest (pension app) BEGap → ETF ✕Free app; ETF 0.4–1% ●●○ watch Closest BE UX analog: reads mypension.be via itsme, states a target, shows the gap → routes to ETFs. BE-only; monetizes via investment product. Could add countries.

Pensioenmanager.be BEBroker-channel tool ✕Free (broker-funded) ●○○ product-seller Reads mypension.be, analyzes P2/P3 for tax-optimized product sales to SME owners (13,800+). Within-Belgium only.

How to read threat: it's the obstacle to Clerio in that market, not company quality — so a strong free government dashboard scores high while a rich robo-advisor scores low. The real ones to watch are the well-funded local gap-tools that already have the UX and just need more countries (Easyvest, Warren, PensionPilot); product-sellers have structural reasons not to build aggregation. No player in any market aggregates + calculates multi-country pensions.
