Clerio · Competitive Landscape

Who Clerio actually competes against

The field mapped by job, not by category. Clerio's job is to unify a person's pension entitlements across several countries and show the retirement-income gap. Almost every "competitor" does one slice of that — one country, one pillar, or balances without a projection.

No product does Clerio's actual job: verified, calculated, multi-country pension aggregation ending in a gap. The whitespace is real — the true competitor is inertia and free single-country government tools, not another app.

What that means: differentiation isn't the problem — activation and trust are. Clerio wins by doing the multi-country state-pension calculation no one else will, and by being the unbiased first step the commissioned-adviser market structurally can't be.

Moat & threats

Structural tailwinds

  • Switzerland has no dashboard — public or private. A core market with no free incumbent to fight. verified
  • FIDA excludes Pillar-2 from mandatory open-data scope (~2026) — the messiest pillar for FR/CH/LU careers stays manual even after open banking, protecting the hard-aggregation moat. verified
  • State-pension calculation from contribution history exists in no tool surveyed — Clerio's defensible core.

Watch-items (years off)

  • ETS adds CH/LU + projection → erodes the pure-aggregation wedge; differentiation shifts to planning/gap.
  • Horizon65 ships cross-border — but its PEPP bet rests on a framework with ~2 providers and ~9% awareness.
  • FIDA final text pulls Pillar-2 back into scope during trilogue → would lower the moat.

Why not PEPP?

The EU already tried to solve fragmentation by manufacturing a new portable product — the Pan-European Personal Pension Product (live 2022). It has largely failed: ~2 authorised providers (Finax, LifeGoals), ~91% consumer non-awareness, killed by a bundled 1% fee cap and no tax harmonisation. Reform ("EuroPension") is pre-trilogue. Two implications: Switzerland is structurally outside PEPP (no EEA residence → no PEPP, ever), so a PEPP-based rival can't even represent a Swiss career; and PEPP is a cautionary tale for the "new product" strategy — the achievable win is the aggregation-and-clarity layer, not a new instrument. verified
