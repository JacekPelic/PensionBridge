# Foundation — Canon

*Phase 2 of the Prevista/Steadwell project. Started 2026-06-27.*

This is the **serious base**: concrete, sourced, and graded. It exists to be challenged. Nothing lives here until it has been re-earned from evidence — a Phase-1 (Discovery) belief is a *hypothesis*, not a fact, until promoted into this folder.

---

## The two rules

1. **Promotion gate.** A claim enters Foundation only by being re-verified against a source, or consciously adopted as a tracked ASSUMPTION. The default state of every Discovery-era claim is *not yet promoted*.
2. **Precedence.** On any conflict between Foundation and Discovery (`retir-ai/docs/`, the deck, the old GTM/funding docs), **Foundation wins.** Discovery is a source of leads, not truth.

## Grading legend

Every claim in a Foundation doc carries a status tag:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Backed by a primary source or first-party data. Cite it inline. |
| **ESTIMATE** | Reasoned and (ideally) ranged, but not sourced. Honest guess. |
| **ASSUMPTION** | A deliberate bet we are carrying until tested. The thing diligence attacks. |
| **OPEN** | A known unknown. Flagged for work, not yet answered. |

Anything not VERIFIED is fair game. The point of grading is to make *load-bearing-but-unproven* claims visible at a glance — to ourselves and to anyone challenging us.

Format for a claim line:
`STATUS — claim — source/basis — date — owner`
