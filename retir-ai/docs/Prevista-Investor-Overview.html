Business model

Prevista unifies fragmented pension data across state, workplace, and personal pillars for internationally-mobile professionals — careers spanning 3+ EU jurisdictions. Two revenue streams compound on the same user base:

Stream 1 · Subscriptions

€4.99/month or €49/year. Annual mix matures to 40% of paid users by Y5. 12% signup→paid conversion in base, 18% in upside.

Stream 2 · Lead generation

0.03% annual trailing commission on aggregate referred capital — a deliberate conservative floor against published 1.0–1.5% LU/FR retail distribution rates. 3.0% of all signups become referred investors (80% gap × 25% click × 15% invest); average €125,000 placement compounds at 9%/year.

Acquisition · seed-stage

Two channels: organic SEO content (compounds with each new country engine) and distribution partnerships (BD-led, ramping from 1 active partner at M7 to 20 by M60 in base). Paid acquisition is held in reserve as a Series A lever — at €4.99 + 12% conversion paid LTV:CAC is 0.49×.

Base vs upside scenarios

Same underlying engine deployment schedule, founder compensation structure, and country-launch costs across both scenarios. The variables that diverge are conversion (signup→paid), organic reach, churn, and partnership velocity.

Founder economics & bootstrap thesis

Two co-founders, both working from day 1. Salary draws are staggered to match the operational reality of a launch: BD becomes the binding constraint earlier (post-launch distribution), tech defers because they have higher pain tolerance for low-pay phase and the patterned engine-build cadence sustains.

BD co-founder

  • Full-time from M7. First paycheck M8 (€3K/month — ramen).
  • Step-up to €5K/month once subMRR > €8K (M20 base / M16 upside).
  • Step-up to €7K/month once subMRR > €25K (M29 base / M22 upside).

Technical co-founder

  • Defers salary until subMRR > €25K (~M29 base / M22 upside).
  • Joins at €5K/month, steps up to €7K once subMRR > €60K (~M45 base / M30 upside).

Total founder cost

Y1-Y5 founder compensation totals €497K (base) / €584K (upside) — split across two people over 5 years, this lands at sub-market LU professional rates and is fully funded from operating cashflow after M28.
