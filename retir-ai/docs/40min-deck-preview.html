Hard problems we navigated 2 min · slide 8 / 11

Three decisions that came close to going the other way.

1 · Pricing model — freemium with monetised free users

Considered
  • Pure paid (no free tier) — €4.99/mo from day one
  • Per-document pricing
  • Premium-only with advisor — €499/yr
  • Freemium with profitable free tier
Pressure-tested
Each non-freemium variant strangles Stream 2. Lead-gen needs the biggest possible signup base — the 3% conversion compounds on cumulative signups, paying or not.
Decided
Freemium. Free-user AI cost ~€0.04/mo; their share of Stream 2 dominates that. The free tier is a feature of the lead-gen business, not a marketing cost.

2 · Germany at M13, not M1

Considered
Greedy TAM-maximising ordering puts DE at M1 (largest single-country corridor unlock).
Pressure-tested
Engineering capacity. DE adds Riester / Rürup / bAV complexity at thinnest bandwidth. Worse — DE at M1 dribbles the unlock across Y1.
Decided
Hold DE to M13. Concentrates the unlock into a single Y2 wave with PL/BE/NL/RO/AT. Addressable: 1.4M → 7.3M in twelve months.

3 · One-time paid services killed

Considered
Power of attorney drafting · pension pay-gap analysis · jurisdictional consolidation. Mid-three-figures per engagement. Real demand.
Pressure-tested
Human-time-intensive, jurisdiction-specific. Margin per hour caps the business at consulting-firm size; splits founder attention from the platform.
Decided
No paid services. Where users need it, we route to partners — the referral fee is Stream 2.

What we don't know yet 1 min · slide 9 / 11

Three things we don't know yet.

Question 1

Will paid conversion hold at 12%?

Stream 1's load-bearing assumption. At 8% conversion, Y5 ARR drops ~33%.

Resolution · M6-M9. Luxembourg's first cohort post-launch is the truth.

Question 2

Does 3% of signups actually convert to a referred investment with €125K avg capital?

Stream 2's load-bearing assumption. Three sub-rates compounded (80% × 25% × 15%) — never measured for this exact funnel.

Resolution · M9-M15. Capital under referral starts compounding when the first partner integration routes real flow.

Question 3

Does "guidance, not advice" hold across seven regulators by Y2?

GDPR is solved. The investment-advice perimeter is not. CSSF · AMF · FINMA · BaFin · CNMV · CMVM · FCA each have different perimeters. A single classification of "investment advice" pauses that engine.

Resolution · per engine, at engine launch. First regulatory contact happens before each country goes live.

These resolve in the first eighteen months. What we learn changes the trajectory — for better or worse. Which brings us to what we're asking for.
