Quick wins — start here
🇫🇷 French Relevé de Carrière
Log in to lassuranceretraite.fr with FranceConnect → "Mon relevé de carrière" → Télécharger.
Instant – 5 minEasy
If you can't log in: create a FranceConnect account via impots.gouv.fr with your numéro fiscal — about 5 minutes.
🇫🇷 Agirc-Arrco complementary pension
Log in to agirc-arrco.fr → "Mon espace personnel" (same FranceConnect login) → download relevé de points.
Instant – 5 minEasy
🇨🇭 Swiss Pillar 3a (Säule 3a)
Log in to each 3a provider (PostFinance, VIAC, Finpension…) → download the annual attestation.
InstantEasy
A little more effort
🇨🇭 Swiss AHV/AVS career extract
Order at ahv-iv.ch (or avs-ai.ch) → "Individual account" → "Order an extract". Arrives by post.
1–2 weeksEasy
🇱🇺 Luxembourg Extrait de Carrière
Log in to guichet.lu with LuxTrust → Social security → Pension → Career extract.
2–3 weeksEasy
If LuxTrust expired: call CNAP at +352 22 41 41-1 and request by phone — they'll post it.
🇱🇺 Luxembourg employer pension (RCP)
Check your annual salary statement under "pension complémentaire employeur"; ask HR for the current statement.
1–3 weeksModerate
Worth the effort — the "found money" ones
🇨🇭 Swiss workplace pension (Vorsorgeausweis / BVG)
Find your last Vorsorgeausweis, or search zentralstelle.ch with your AHV number for transferred capital.
1–4 weeksMay need help
If zentralstelle finds nothing: call Auffangeinrichtung BVG at +41 41 799 75 75 — unclaimed benefits default there.
🇨🇭 Swiss vested benefits (Freizügigkeitskonto)
Search zentralstelle.ch with your AHV number for forgotten 2nd-pillar capital, then request a Freizügigkeitsausweis.
2–6 weeksMay need help
