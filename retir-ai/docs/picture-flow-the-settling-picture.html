Tracing your Swiss vested benefits
✶ Tightens your Swiss band by ±€300/mo
Time1–2 weeks CostFree EffortEasy LanguageDE / FR / EN
  1. Go to the 2nd Pillar Central Office (LOB Guarantee Fund) at sfbvg.ch — they hold records of forgotten vested-benefit accounts.
  2. Download the search request form and enter your AVS/AHV number (the 13-digit 756.xxxx number on your old Swiss payslips).
  3. Send it by post or email. They search every foundation in Switzerland for accounts in your name.
  4. You receive a reply by post naming the foundation(s) holding your money and the current balance.
Tip: Many expats discover a vested-benefit account they'd entirely forgotten — this is the step that most often raises the estimate rather than just narrowing it.
Lost your AVS number? Your old Swiss employer or the cantonal compensation office (Ausgleichskasse) can retrieve it from your name and date of birth — no document needed to start.
