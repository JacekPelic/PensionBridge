Market & sizing · the engine compounds ~1:30 · slide 7A / 17

Country engines compound.
They don't run in parallel.

Pension data is country-anchored. Mats's full picture requires every country he's ever worked in. When we add a new engine, two things happen at once.

⊕ Additive New residents become addressable.

Every person already living in the new country can now build a complete picture for the first time. The engine reaches them on day one.

+ Germany at M13. Europe's largest single labour market becomes addressable on day one.
⊗ Compounding Existing users get a completed picture.

Every prior user with history in the new country sees their picture upgrade from incomplete to whole. Retention deepens. Cancellation reasons evaporate.

+ Germany at M13. Every existing LU/FR/PT/ES/IT user who ever worked in Germany gets a full picture for the first time.
→
Each new engine is a multiplier on every prior cohort, not just a flag for new ones. One hundred and forty thousand at M1 reaches twelve million by Y5 because the graph compounds — not because we sell harder.

Expansion is greedy under engineering capacity (one new engine every 2–3 months). Germany is held to M13 deliberately — its arrival concentrates the unlock into a single 12-month wave instead of dribbling it across Y1.
