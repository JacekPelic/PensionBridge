*Working draft, 2026-04-27. Companion to `relocation-agency-channel-plan-2026-04-19.md`. Commercial terms are set by the simplified single-tier offer; this doc is purely targeting.*

## 1. Approach

- LU-active firms only.
- Spuerkeess's published "trusted partner" list (Apr 2026) used as a curated trust signal — 5 of the 8 candidates below are on it.
- Volume estimates are heuristic (longevity + headcount + service breadth proxies). Founder/operator to refine.
- Decision-makers are public (LinkedIn / Chamber listings / press); no proprietary data.
- Three waves: Wave 1 founder-led LU specialists; Wave 2 broader / mover-led / immigration-led; Defer global DSPs.
