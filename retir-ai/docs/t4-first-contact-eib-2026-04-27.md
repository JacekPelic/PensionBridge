# T4 First Contact — European Investment Bank (Luxembourg)

*2026-04-27. Outcome of the T4 channel discussion. This is the single first-contact target the discussion converged on, with offer and expected return.*

## Why EIB first

- **LU HQ** — geographically aligned with T1 work and founder network.
- **Pilot-sized:** ~4,000 staff vs. Commission's 32,000. Big enough to matter, small enough that one workshop has measurable impact.
- **Profile A by definition:** EIB recruits internationally; staff routinely have 3-jurisdiction careers and high salaries. This is our exact target user.
- **Cross-border pension complexity is *higher* not lower at EIB.** EIB runs its own occupational pension scheme (separate from the EU Staff Pension Scheme). EIB staff carry the EIB scheme + prior national rights from FR/DE/IT/ES home countries. Multi-pillar claim-timing is a real, unsolved question.
- **Reachable buyer confirmed:** EIB's **College of Staff Representatives** (RP/SRs) at LU HQ exists, has operational support staff, and explicitly "interacts with active or retired EIB staff members." This is the staff-committee route — 60–120 day cycle, no procurement.
- **Halo effect:** "Delivered at the EIB" is a credible reference for the European Commission, ECJ, and Court of Auditors approaches later.

## What we offer

A **60-min cross-border pre-retirement workshop**, delivered in person at EIB Luxembourg (or hybrid).

**Content:**
- The interaction between EIB's occupational pension scheme and prior national rights (FR/DE/IT/ES dominant priors).
- Claim-timing scenarios — when to take the EIB pension vs prior national pensions for tax-optimal sequencing.
- Live demo of Prevista consolidating fragmented pre-EIB pension data.
- 15 min Q&A.

**Take-home assets:**
- "Your pensions checklist" PDF, co-branded EIB Staff Reps + Prevista.
- **6-month free Pro** for any EIB staff member who attends, gated by EIB email.
- Recording + slide deck for staff unable to attend live.

**What this is NOT:**
- Not regulated advice — orientation only (same scope as the T1 CSSF letter).
- No money flows in either direction.
- No staff list handed to Prevista (they promote, we host signups via co-branded landing page).
- No exclusivity demanded — Staff Reps are free to do other workshops.

## What we expect in return

**From the partner (EIB Staff Reps):**
- Slot for the workshop in their member programme (90 min total: 60 workshop + 15 Q&A + 15 buffer).
- Promotion via Staff Reps newsletter, intranet, and member email — at least once pre-event and once post-event with the recording link.
- Room and facilities at EIB Luxembourg.
- Co-branded framing as "Joint EIB Staff Reps + Prevista" session.

**Expected numbers, single pilot workshop:**

| Metric | Realistic range | Source of estimate |
|---|---|---|
| EIB staff age 50+ | ~1,200–1,600 | 30–40% of 4,000 staff |
| Within 5 years of claim | ~200–400 | Standard EU institutional retirement bell-curve |
| Workshop attendance (in-room) | 50–100 | Typical staff-rep workshop turnout |
| Free Pro signups (attendees + post-event newsletter reach) | 60–150 | 40–60% in-room conversion + 30–50 newsletter signups |
| Paid conversions year 1 | 15–35 | 25–30% trial-to-paid (T1 baseline) |
| Recurring quarterly slot | High likelihood if attendance >50 | Staff Reps' decision criterion is member value, not metrics |

**12-month outlook if pilot succeeds:** Recurring quarterly workshop = 60–140 paid EIB users in year 1, plus a case study that opens Commission, ECJ, and Court of Auditors doors.

## First-contact mechanic

**Target:** Chair of the EIB College of Staff Representatives (or secretariat email if chair is non-public).

**Approach:** Cold email + LinkedIn approach in parallel. 3-paragraph email:
1. Why we're writing them specifically (cross-border pension complexity is hardest for EIB staff with multi-jurisdiction careers).
2. The workshop concept and what's in it for EIB staff (a pre-retirement session that actually addresses the cross-border question, not just the EIB scheme mechanics).
3. Ask for a 30-min call.

**Open dependency:** the current Staff Reps chair name + contact has not yet been verified — needs a 10-min LinkedIn / EIB intranet check before the email goes out.

## What this discussion *didn't* lock in (deliberate)

- The 60-min workshop content needs an outline (separate work; ~half day).
- The take-home PDF needs design (template; reusable across EU institutions).
- The CSSF letter scope check (does the existing letter cover educational workshops at a public-sector employer, or do we need a small extension?).
- A second EU-institution target queued behind EIB (likely the European Court of Auditors — smaller still, also LU-anchored, as the post-EIB second pilot).
