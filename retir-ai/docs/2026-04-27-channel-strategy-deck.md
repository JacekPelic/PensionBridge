## 1 / Framework

**Profile A:** internationally-mobile EU professional, 35–60, career across 3+ jurisdictions.

**5 acquisition triggers** — moments when Profile A becomes acquirable:

| | Trigger |
|---|---|
| T1 | Cross-border move in-flight |
| T2 | Leaving a country (forgotten pensions) |
| T3 | Annual tax / contribution calendar |
| T4 | Approaching retirement while mobile |
| T5 | Cross-border property purchase |

Trigger × channel = planning matrix. Same trigger, different channel options.

## 6 / Key decisions made today

1. **Partnership logic must lead with value-exchange, not mechanics.** Three-line check before naming firms: pain we solve / value they send / scale-clean.
2. **Cross-border IFAs are partial competitors, not partners.** They substitute Prevista's upstream data-consolidation work; commission IFAs structurally need users to stay confused.
3. **T3 partnership angle is CH-only.** LU's 111bis market is insurance-led; no digital partner exists.
4. **Affiliate fees are appropriate for product-distributor partners (T3 / VIAC)**, not for service partners (T1 / relocation, T4 / institutions).
5. **For LU launch, the partnership effort is T1 + T4. Everything else is content/SEO.** Cleaner operating picture than running 5 channels in parallel.
