# Prevista — T4 Pre-Retirement Channel Frame (working draft v1)

*2026-04-27. Companion to the T1 relocation-agency channel plan. T4 = "Approaching retirement while mobile" — the #2 trigger for the first-1000 plan, highest LTV per user.*

## 1. Why this isn't a copy-paste of T1

T1's relocation-agency offer (free Pro for referred assignees, no rev share, no data exchange) works because:
- The agency's customer is a **corporate HR**, not the assignee.
- Pension orientation is a free service-line differentiator, paid for in the corporate contract.
- No regulatory advice surface — agency hands off to "their tax advisor / wealth manager."

T4 inverts every one of those:
- The IFA's customer **is the Profile-A user directly**.
- IFA's revenue model is fee-on-AUM or commission — pension orientation is upstream of their advice, not a service-line.
- IFAs are regulated advisors (FCA, CSSF, AMF). Sending users to specific advisors crosses different regulatory thresholds than sending users to relocation services.
- Brand-association risk is real: the cross-border IFA segment includes operators with regulatory baggage (deVere, parts of Holborn). Wrong partners damage Prevista's "verified, not sold" positioning.

**Implication:** the offer structure, brand screen, and regulatory framing all need fresh thinking *before* naming a target list.

## 2. T4 has three sub-ecosystems — drilling IFAs first

| Sub-ecosystem | Why it matters | When to drill |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Cross-border IFAs serving EU expats** | Largest commercial vector; direct two-way referral mechanics | **Now (this doc)** |
| **B. EU institution pre-retirement programmes** | EU Commission, Parliament, Court of Auditors, EIB run formal seminars. B2B2C, no commercial complexity, but long contract cycles | After IFA pilot has 1 signed partner |
| **C. Older expat associations + niche specialists** | Trust-led, low cost, low volume per partner; serves the "I'm 60 and confused" segment | Opportunistic; not a formal channel push |

Private bank international wealth desks (Quintet, BIL, BdL, UBS GWM expat) overlap A — different sales motion, parked as a separate sub-track.

## 3. Decision required: IFA partnership structure

| Model | What we give | What partner gives | Brand fit | Regulatory weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Two-way referral + advisor seat** | Co-branded "trusted advisor" listing for users who hit "I need advice"; view-only Prevista access for the IFA's existing clients (with client consent) | Mention Prevista in onboarding/advice flow; warm intros to peer advisors | **Strong** — preserves "we orient, you advise" positioning | Light; advisor listing is curation not introduction-with-fee |
| **2. White-label data layer** | IFA gets co-branded Prevista access for their entire book at flat licence fee | Annual licence fee + product feedback | Risk: dilutes Prevista brand if partner is downmarket | Light |
| **3. Affiliate fee on AUM** | Referral fee on AUM that converts to advice | Cash payment per converted user | **Kills credibility** — incompatible with "verified, not sold" | Heavy in most jurisdictions |

**Recommendation: Option 1.** Mirrors the relocation-agency simplicity. Prevista stays positioned as the upstream orientation tool; IFA stays in their lane on regulated advice. No money flows. The "advisor seat" is a real value exchange — IFAs spend hours building cross-border pension snapshots that Prevista produces in minutes.

## 4. Brand screen (filter out before research)

To partner with Prevista, an IFA must meet:
- **Tier-1 regulator:** FCA (UK), CSSF (LU), AMF (FR), FINMA (CH), CONSOB (IT), CMVM (PT), CNMV (ES), or equivalent EEA passport. No Dubai-only / offshore-only.
- **Fee-based or chartered fee-and-commission.** No pure-commission/IBC-product-pushing models.
- **No live regulatory action in last 36 months.**
- **Public adviser team page** (transparency proxy — operators with regulatory baggage often hide their teams).

**Pre-emptive exclusions:** deVere Group, Holborn Assets entities tied to UAE-only licences, Globaleye, generic "Wealth Solutions"-branded operators.

**Pre-emptive inclusions to research:** Blevins Franks, Spectrum IFA Group, AES International, Chase Buchanan, Forth Capital, Black Tower Financial Management, Beacon Global Wealth Management, Skybound Wealth.

## 5. Regulatory dependency

The CSSF letter that gates T1 covers Prevista's status as "not regulated advice" *for orientation*. T4 raises a separate question: when Prevista lists named advisors to a user, is that a regulated introduction? In LU, the CSSF answer matters. In FR, AMF rules on tied-agent / mandataire status apply. UK FCA rules on introductions/Article 25 RAO are well-defined.

**Action:** before signing a Wave-1 IFA, get a one-pager legal opinion (1–2 hours of counsel time) covering: (a) "trusted advisor directory" mechanic, (b) "advisor seat" data-access mechanic, (c) jurisdictions covered by Wave 1.

This should run **in parallel** with firm-research, not block it.
